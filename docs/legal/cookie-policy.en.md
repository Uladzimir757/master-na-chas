**Cookie Policy — ZR**

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies (and similar technologies — local storage, session tokens) are small pieces of data stored on your device that, among other things, remember your preferences and keep you logged in.

## 2. Cookies we currently use

| Category | Example | Purpose | Retention | Consent required? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Necessary | login session token (Client/Master) | keeping you logged in, security | until logout / session expiry | No — necessary for the service (Art. 6(1)(b)/(f) GDPR) |
| Necessary | selected interface language (pl/ru/uk/en) | remembering your language preference | [●] | No |
| Functional | *(not currently used — reserved for future use, e.g. remembering search filters)* | — | — | Yes, once implemented |
| Analytics | *(not currently used — ZR does not yet use Google Analytics or similar tools)* | — | — | Yes, once implemented |
| Marketing | *(not currently used — ZR does not use advertising pixels, e.g. Meta Pixel, Google Ads)* | — | — | Yes, once implemented |

## 3. Managing consent

If we later add functional, analytics, or marketing cookies, we will show a consent banner on first visit letting you accept or reject them by category. Consent can be withdrawn at any time via [●: footer link / account settings].

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) — blocking necessary cookies may make the Platform hard or impossible to use (e.g. you won't be able to log in).
